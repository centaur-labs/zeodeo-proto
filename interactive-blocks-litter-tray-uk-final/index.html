Smart Solutions

4 Biggest Litter Tray Problems

Odour, dirty cleaning, scattered litter and dust - ZeoDeo is designed to solve all four.

01

No More Litter Tray Smell

Odour is trapped at the source, not masked.

ZeoDeo pellets are made from porous zeolite with an enormous microscopic pore structure. Odour-causing molecules are captured inside these pores, providing powerful, long-lasting odour control.

✓700-800 m²/cm³ microporous zeolite surface • Long-lasting odour control
02

Cleaning Made Simple

No more dealing with urine-soaked litter.

In ordinary litter, urine stays inside the litter itself, creating wet, dirty material that needs frequent scooping and periodic complete replacement. With ZeoDeo, urine passes through the pellet layer into a separate superabsorbent pad below, keeping liquid away from the upper litter layer.

Simply pull out the pad, fold it, dispose of it and insert a fresh one

✓Pad lasts 7 days • No more weekly full washes
The pellets handle odour. The pad handles liquid.
03

Litter Stays in the Tray

Less litter under your feet. Less mess around your home.

Large smooth ZeoDeo pellets are much less likely to become trapped between paws or cling to fur than fine traditional litter. Even when an enthusiastic cat kicks a few pellets outside the tray, they stay solid and intact instead of turning into gritty debris when stepped on.

✓Large 5-6mm pellets • Easy to pick up
Traditional litter (silica gel) ZeoDeo pellets (large & smooth)
Tracks everywhere Stays in the tray
04

Virtually Dust-Free

Cleaner paws. Cleaner surfaces. Cleaner home.

Fine traditional litter can release dust when poured, scratched and carried out of the tray. ZeoDeo's solid pellets create virtually no loose litter dust during everyday use.

No clouds of litter dust. No dusty paw prints across your floors and furniture.

✓Virtually dust-free everyday use
Traditional litter (silica gel) ZeoDeo pellets
Visible dust clouds Clean & fresh air
